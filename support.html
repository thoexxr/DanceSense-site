DanceSense 用你手机上的计算能力给舞蹈打分：选一段自己的视频，App 在本机提取人体骨架， 和内置标准示范逐帧对齐，输出 0–100 的标准度分数、分部位误差和最需要改进的关节。 所有处理都在设备本地完成，视频不会离开你的手机。

三步上手

  1. 首页选「标准度测试」（与内置标准示范对比）或「舞蹈视频分析」（分析你自己的视频）。
  2. 点「选择视频」，挑一段单人、全身入镜、竖屏的片段。
  3. 等报告出来：总分与分级、各部位误差条、最差关节清单，还可以在结果页生成成绩卡分享。

常见问题

分数是怎么算的？

App 用 Apple Vision 在设备上提取每帧的 17 个人体关键点，把你的动作序列与标准示范做 DTW 时间对齐， 再逐关节比较角度与位置误差并加权成 0–100 分，同时给出「高度一致 / 较为接近 / 差异较大」等分级。

我的视频会被上传吗？

不会。骨架提取、模型推理、对齐和评分全部在设备本地完成，App 没有服务器，也不含第三方统计或广告 SDK。详见隐私政策。

提示「未检测到完整人体」或分数很低怎么办？

通常是取景问题。请保证：画面里只有你一个人、头和脚都在框内、身体不要被裁切、尽量竖屏正对镜头、 光线充足且避免强逆光。多人同框、半身入镜、横屏远景都会让骨架提取失败或严重失真。

视频里有前奏、走位或结尾鞠躬，会影响分数吗？

会。对齐是基于整段视频的，多余的动作会被当成错误匹配。建议在相册里先把真正跳舞的那一段剪出来再选择 （十几秒到一分钟最合适），分数会更贴近真实水平。

DanceSense scores your dancing with the computing power already in your phone. Pick a video, and the app extracts body poses on-device, aligns them frame by frame against a built-in reference routine, and returns a 0–100 accuracy score, per-body-part errors, and the joints that need the most work. Everything runs locally — your video never leaves the device.

Getting started

  1. On the home screen choose Accuracy Test (compare against the built-in reference) or Dance Video Analysis (analyse your own clip).
  2. Tap Choose Video and pick a clip that shows one person, full body, in portrait.
  3. Read the report: total score and grade, per-part error bars, the worst joints, plus a shareable score card.

FAQ

How is the score computed?

Apple Vision extracts 17 body keypoints on-device. Your motion sequence is time-aligned to the reference with DTW, then joint angles and positions are compared, weighted, and turned into a 0–100 score with a qualitative grade (highly consistent / close / noticeably different).

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Pose extraction, model inference, alignment and scoring all happen on-device; there is no server and no analytics or advertising SDK. See the privacy policy.

It says no full body was detected, or the score is very low.

That is usually framing. Make sure only you are in frame, head to feet are visible, nothing is cropped, hold the camera in portrait facing you, and use good light without strong backlight. Several people, a half-body shot, or a wide landscape shot will break pose extraction.

My clip has an intro, walking around, or a bow at the end.

Those parts distort the score, because alignment covers the whole clip. Trim the clip down to the part where you actually dance (roughly 15 seconds to one minute) before selecting it.
